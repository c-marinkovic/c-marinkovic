# Cynthia Marinković

MFA in Visual Arts and Creative Technologist

Studio Kovic: art direction for boutique brands, from oil painting to the entire digital ecosystem. Avant-garde design with an author's vision, powered by artificial intelligence and AI agents: immersive 3D web experiences, AI-generated imagery, interactive brand avatars and agent systems for content creation, including the production of assets for digital marketing campaigns on Meta, YouTube and Google.

## Selected projects

**[KOVIC](https://studiokovic.eu)**
Main website of my art direction studio.

**[KOVIC Vault](https://vaultkovic.netlify.app/)**
Interactive 3D logo you can rotate with click and drag, set over moving video and an original soundtrack.

**[IMPURA — Between Elsewheres](https://impurawebinmersiva.netlify.app/)**
Immersive 3D web experience for a fragrance concept. A monochrome night landscape built with Three.js, where the scent lives between two languages and one universe. Art direction by KOVIC.
[Code](https://github.com/c-marinkovic/Impurawebinmersiva)

**[KOVIC Motion](https://animationkovic.netlify.app/)**
Animated version of the studio site with glitch transitions and motion design as brand identity.

**[Interactive Brand Agents](https://studiokovic.eu/agentes-interactivos-de-marca-kovic)**
Conversational AI avatars that speak for a brand in real time. Each avatar's LLM is trained on the brand's own knowledge. Visitors can talk to three different characters directly on the page.
